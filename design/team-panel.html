My fantasy team panel

Sits to the right of the chat in the main window. It shows the roster as set in Sleeper, never a recommendation.

  • Slots follow the league: this sample is an IDP league (DL, LB, DB). A non-IDP league shows DEF and no IDP slots.
  • Starters, then bench, then injured reserve, in Sleeper's order.
  • Each player: name, position, NFL team, injury flag (letters and words, never color alone), game time before kickoff, points once played.
  • The warning above the roster appears only when a starter is OUT, IR, PUP, SUS, DNR or NA, or a starting slot is empty.
  • The matchup shows this week's score for both fantasy teams. Before kickoff the scores show a dash and the start time. The three states follow matchup.state: not_started, live, final.
  • An empty starting slot (player: null) shows as Empty slot, and the warning says so.
  • Chips from injury_status: Questionable Q, Doubtful D, Out OUT, IR IR, PUP PUP, Sus SUS, DNR DNR, NA NA; null (healthy) shows no chip. Yellow = might play, red = will not play, gray = not on the active roster. Hover gives the full words.
  • No matchup (matchup is null): a fantasy playoff bye or an eliminated fantasy team. The roster still shows.
  • Refresh failed: the stored data stays visible, the banner says how old it is.
  • Panel scrolls on its own; its header stays visible.
