Segment-specific weights¶

Per segment, on a grid of nflverse weights 0 to 1:

  • argmin_w: the weight with the lowest 2024 MAE, and what it does in 2025. It is noisy: large weights have wide intervals.
  • qual_w: the weight with the lowest 2024 MAE among weights whose 2024 paired interval is below 0 (none = no segment weight is supported). Its 2025 result is the out-of-sample check. A segment "holds" if both intervals sit below 0.

With about 30 segments, one or two false positives at 5% are expected. The qualified-weight rule was added after seeing that argmin picked wide-interval weights in a first pass of this notebook, so treat a "holds" as a candidate to re-test on 2026.

Conclusion¶

History across seasons. The nflverse source now averages the last 4 games across seasons (last season's games count half; tuned on 2024: 4 games, weight 0.5 beat 1.0 and 6 or 8 games by under 0.003 points). Weeks 1-2 now have nflverse rows for 99% of players, and the career-games segments below replace the old "games this season" ones.

Cold start is not where nflverse helps.

  • 1-3 career games (n=132 in 2024, 164 in 2025): nflverse alone is 1.03 points worse than Sleeper in both seasons; the blend is -0.04 [-0.13, +0.04] and +0.01 [-0.06, +0.08]. 0 career games (rookies' first game, n=42 and 37) has no nflverse row, so the blend equals Sleeper.
  • New NFL team since last season (n=776 and 680, shown separately): nflverse alone +0.36 and +0.41 worse; blend -0.02 [-0.05, -0.00] and -0.01 [-0.04, +0.01]. Weeks 1-2: nflverse alone +0.61 and +0.80 worse; blend -0.01 and +0.01.
  • Rookies, missed games, snap-share trend, position and Sleeper tier: nflverse alone is worse everywhere; the blend is within +-0.02 with intervals that include 0.

Weights tuned on 2024, checked on 2025. Five segments have a weight whose 2024 interval is below 0; two hold in 2025:

  • Weeks 13-17 at 0.1 (the shipped weight): -0.022 in 2024 and -0.020 in 2025, both intervals below 0.
  • Sleeper had over-projected the player over the previous 4 weeks, at 0.3: -0.074 [..., -0.005] in 2024 and -0.071 [..., -0.005] in 2025. Where Sleeper had under-projected him, nflverse hurts at every weight above 0 in both seasons.

A joint rule (0.3 when Sleeper over-projected lately, 0.0 when under-projected, else 0.1): against Sleeper-only -0.017 [-0.032, -0.002] in 2024 (in sample) and -0.024 [-0.041, -0.007] in 2025 (out of sample); against the shipped blend -0.010 [-0.022, +0.002] and -0.019 [-0.031, -0.007]. The shipped 0.9/0.1 blend alone is -0.007 and -0.005, intervals that include 0. The shipped weights stay as they are: the rule needs a new input (Sleeper's own recent errors, which needs stored point-in-time Sleeper rows) and its 2024 number is in sample, so it is a candidate for the owner to decide.

Caveats. About 30 segments were examined. The qualified-weight rule was added after a first pass that used the plain 2024 minimum, which picks large weights with wide intervals. The recent-error feature uses Sleeper's stored post-game weekly rows.
